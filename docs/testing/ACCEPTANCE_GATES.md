# Acceptance gates

## Public test network

- Four independent witness organizations and seven independent evidence
  validator/reviewer organizations.
- No open Critical/High finding without authorized residual-risk acceptance.
- Split-view, partition, replay, rollback, revocation, sandbox, load, restore,
  and key-compromise exercises pass.
- Clients verify exact bytes, evidence, inclusion, consistency, freshness, and
  revocation.

## Production candidate

- External contract, protocol, sandbox, and supply-chain reviews complete.
- Durable no-drop queues, multi-domain evidence retention, regional restore,
  and 24/7 incident ownership demonstrated.
- Real SBOM/provenance and risk-based reproducible-build evidence available.
- Revenue/budget covers 18 months of base operations without token appreciation.
- Legal/privacy review and public residual-risk report complete.
